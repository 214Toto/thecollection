Histology (MBS 200)

Microscopic study of tissues and cellular structures to understand the fundamental building blocks of the body.

Embryology (MBS 200)

Exploration of prenatal development, from gametogenesis to the formation of complex organ systems.

Gross Anatomy (MBS 200)

Detailed study of human physical structures through systemic and regional dissection and observation.

Physiology (MBS 210)

Investigating the mechanical, physical, and biochemical functions of living systems and organs.

Pathology (MBS 220)

The study of the causes and effects of diseases, bridging clinical practice and basic science.

Therapeutics (MBS 230)

Focusing on the application of drugs and treatments to maintain health and combat disease states.

Biochemistry (MBS 230)

Biochemical processes of the human body, focusing on metabolism, enzymes, and molecular mechanisms of disease.

Lab Science (MBS 240)

Technical principles and practical applications of laboratory methods in a clinical setting.

Diagnostics (MBS 240)

The art and science of identifying conditions through clinical examination and technological analysis.

Clinical Science (MCS 200)

Integrating medical knowledge with patient care, ethics, and bedside clinical skills.

Public Health (MCS 210)

Study of population-level health trends, disease prevention, and health policy management.

Social Medicine (MCS 210)

Understanding how social and economic factors impact health outcomes and medical practice.

Collection of previous examination questions for revision, practice, and exam-oriented preparation.
